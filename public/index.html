How sync works

Everything you do is saved on this device first, instantly, with or without signal. Sync sends your changes to the server, then fetches everyone else's. Until you sync, other people on the board don't see your changes — and you don't see theirs.

If someone else finished or deleted a task you changed offline, your change to it is dropped and you're told which one.

Reminders

Reminders not arriving when the app is closed?

Reminders are alarms held by Android itself, so they fire whether or not HomeBoard is running — including after you swipe it away. Some phones (Realme, Oppo, Xiaomi, Samsung, OnePlus) put apps to sleep aggressively and will hold them back anyway.

On those, allow HomeBoard to run in the background:

  1. Settings → Apps → HomeBoard → Battery → allow background activity, or set it to Unrestricted / Don't optimise.
  2. On Realme and Oppo, also turn on Auto-launch (sometimes under App management → Auto startup).
  3. Settings → Apps → HomeBoard → Alarms & reminders → allow.

Danger zone

Deletes your account, the boards only you are on, and every task on them. Boards you share with other people are not touched — hand those over first.

HomeBoard · when a task is marked done its details, steps and notes are deleted for good — only the name is kept.

Waking HomeBoard up

The server goes to sleep when nobody's using it. This takes up to a minute, and only on the first open.
